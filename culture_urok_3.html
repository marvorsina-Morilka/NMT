4 Sixto Rodriguez · reading

Прочитайте і оберіть відповіді. (5 балів)

Sixto Rodriguez is an American singer-songwriter from Detroit. He wrote about his home town, the Vietnam war, civil rights and equality. In South Africa in the 1970s, anti-apartheid supporters related to his lyrics and he became extremely popular — though fans used illegal bootlegs and he received no royalties.
In 1997, Stephen ‘Sugar’ Segerman set up a website ‘The Great Rodriguez Hunt’. Later, Sixto’s daughter found the site and was shocked that her father was a star on the other side of the globe.
His career started in 1968; he made two albums that didn’t sell well and he retired. He worked in construction and factories, ran for mayor twice, and lived simply. The film Searching for Sugar Man won an Oscar in 2013. He still lives in the same house, plays the same guitar, and says success matters because he can share his songs and message.
